To my dearest Bubu,

Happy Diwali, my love. ❤️

I miss you. I still find myself thinking about you, about us, and about all the little things we shared. Even though we still talk, it is so different from how it used to be. I miss our closeness, our conversations, the little moments, and being a part of each other's everyday life. I miss being able to call you mine.

I think I'm writing this because you deserve it more than anyone. You deserve to know what's been going on in my heart, the things I've wanted to say but haven't always found the courage to express. You deserve honesty from me, something I should have given you all along. I don't want to leave these feelings unspoken, especially when you've been someone so incredibly special to me.

I know I hid things from you, and I know I pushed you away. I hurt you, and you never deserved that. My illness was part of what I was going through, but it doesn't excuse the way I made you feel. I'm truly sorry. I regret not being honest with you and not letting you understand what was happening with me.

I'm still struggling with my illness, just as I was before. I haven't suddenly become better, and I don't want to keep pretending that I'm okay or hiding parts of myself anymore. I don't want to keep things from you. I wish I had understood sooner that I could have been honest instead of shutting you out.

I regret pushing you away more than I can explain. I wish I had opened up about what I was going through and allowed you to be there for me. I always trusted you, even when I struggled to share what was happening inside me. Sometimes I wonder how different things might have been if I had only communicated instead of keeping everything inside.

I regret it all so much. Sometimes I wish I could have another chance with you, not to pretend that nothing ever happened, but to show you a different side of me — the Riya who has nothing to hide from you, who can be completely honest with you, and who isn't afraid to let you in. I wish I could give you all the love I have in my heart, the kind of love you have always deserved from me. I wish I could make you feel loved, valued, and appreciated in every way you deserve.

I really need you by my side, Tarun. And I know I can't ask that of you anymore because we're not together like we used to be. I understand that things are different now, and I respect that. But that doesn't change how much I miss your support, your presence, and simply knowing that you were there beside me.

I miss having you as my person — someone I could turn to, tell what was happening, and share the things that were hurting me with. I miss your reassurance and comfort. I'm not saying this to make you responsible for what I'm going through or to ask you to carry my struggles. I just want to be honest about how much I miss you and the closeness we once had. I wish I hadn't made you feel unwanted when all along I never wanted to lose you.

I don't expect you to forget what happened or pretend that nothing hurt. I only hope you can understand what I've been trying to say. You mean so much to me, and what we had was never ordinary to me.

You know, Bubu, no matter how much time passes or how much things change between us, a part of me will always see you as that little boy who used to be so clingy with me, who would do the silliest and stupidest things just to make me smile whenever I was having a bad day. I still remember that side of you so clearly, and it's one of the things I miss the most. You never needed to do anything extraordinary to make me happy. Sometimes, just having you around, being your silly self, was enough to make my worst days feel a little better. And even now, when I think of you, I don't just see the person you've become. I still see that same adorable, silly Bubu who always found a way to make me laugh, even when I didn't feel like smiling. I miss him. I miss that version of us. And I wish I could hold that little boy close to my heart and remind him how much he has always meant to me. ❤️

I love you. I loved you then, I love you now, and I know a part of me will continue to love you. I am genuinely proud of you, Tarun. I hope you achieve every dream you're working towards, keep growing, find happiness, and receive all the beautiful things life has waiting for you.

I'll always be on your side, cheering for you and wishing you the best for as long as I live. Your happiness matters to me, and I hope you never forget how proud I am of the person you are and the person you're becoming.

I wish we could celebrate Diwali together, share the happiness, and make more memories. I wish I hadn't let things come between us, and I wish I had protected what we had. Still, I'm grateful for every moment we shared and for having had someone like you in my life.

I don't know what the future holds, but I know that I still care about you deeply. I miss you, I think of you, and I have so much love for you in my heart.

I know you've been busy with your life, and I understand that you have so much going on. But please don't forget to take care of yourself, okay? Drink enough water, eat your meals on time, get proper sleep, and don't push yourself too hard. I know I might not be there beside you to remind you of these little things anymore, but that doesn't mean I've stopped caring. Sometimes I wish I could still be there to look after you, make sure you're doing okay, and take care of you in all those little ways I always wanted to. I wish I could be the one reminding you to rest when you're tired and making sure you're taking care of yourself. Even from a distance, your health, your happiness, and your well-being will always matter so much to me. ❤️

I know I keep asking about your health, reminding you to take care of yourself, asking if you've eaten, if you've had enough water, or if you've been sleeping properly. And maybe sometimes I sound repetitive, but Bubu, I can't help it. I still worry about you the same way I always did. And sometimes, it genuinely makes me sad knowing that all I can do now is remind you through messages instead of actually being there beside you. I wish I could look after you myself, make sure you're okay, take care of you when you're tired or unwell, and give you the comfort you deserve. It hurts a little knowing I can't do those things anymore, especially when there's still so much love and care for you in my heart. I know things between us aren't the same anymore, and I respect that, but I hope you understand that every little reminder comes from a place of love. I don't ask because I think you can't take care of yourself. I ask because you're still someone incredibly precious to me, and I wish I could do more than just ask if you're okay. ❤️

This Diwali, I pray that you are always happy, healthy, safe, and surrounded by people who love you. May every effort you make bring you closer to your dreams. May your life be filled with peace, light, success, and all the happiness you deserve.

♡   ✦   ♡
Happy Diwali, Tarun.

I hope you know that somewhere, someone is incredibly proud of you, misses you more than words can explain, and wishes nothing but the best for you.

I love you, and I'm always rooting for you.

With love,
Riya
